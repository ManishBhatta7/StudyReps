Have you ever watched a car speed up or a ball fall and wondered how we describe its movement precisely? How far will it go? How fast will it be? Well, that's where the amazing **Equations of Motion** come in!

These are powerful tools in physics that help us understand and predict the motion of objects moving with **constant acceleration**. Let's meet our key players: **u** is your initial velocity, **v** is your final velocity, **a** is the acceleration, **t** stands for time, and **s** represents the displacement.

Now for the equations!

First: **v = u + at**. This equation helps us find the final velocity if we know initial velocity, acceleration, and time.

Next: **s = ut + ½at²**. Perfect for calculating the **displacement** of an object when you know its initial velocity, acceleration, and time.

And finally, a very special one: **v² = u² + 2as**. This is your go-to when you need to find variables like final velocity or displacement, but **time (t) is not given** in the problem, or isn't what you need to find. Super handy for those specific situations!

For example, imagine a car accelerates from 5 m/s to 15 m/s over a certain distance, with an acceleration of 2 m/s². How far did it travel? Notice, we don't have time! This is a perfect case for **v² = u² + 2as**. You can easily calculate the displacement using this equation!

To recap, the three fundamental **Equations of Motion**: **v = u + at**, **s = ut + ½at²**, and **v² = u² + 2as**. They are your go-to tools for analyzing motion with constant acceleration. Master these, and you'll unlock the secrets of movement!
