 # StudyReps: Core Product Vision
*Do one thing perfectly.* 

## 🎯 The Core Problem
Students passively watch educational videos like entertainment. They don't retain information, and they get distracted instantly. Post-pandemic, their reading and writing stamina is fundamentally broken.

## ✨ The Solution
The **Active Video Player**. 
An AI-powered video player that forces active participation and vocalization. 

*   **Vocal-First:** No typing. The student must speak their answers.
*   **Question Locking:** The video literally stops playing. The screen blurs. The AI asks a question about what was just taught. The video *only* resumes when the student explains the concept out loud correctly (The Feynman Technique). 
*   **Infinite Patience:** If the student doesn't know the answer, the AI coaches them through it conversationally. Zero shame.
*   **Absolute Focus:** The environment is laser-focused. No algorithmic feeds. No ads. If the student leaves the app, the video stops, and their momentum is broken.

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 The Business Model (Who Pays)
*   **WhatsApp Parent Bot:** Parents don't want to install another complex dashboard app. They just want a text message on WhatsApp saying: *"Jimmy actively engaged with Biology for 20 minutes today. He successfully answered 4 voice prompts, but lost focus and left the app twice."*

---
*Philosophical Pillar:* We are not building a complex "Learning Management System." We are not building a pomodoro timer. We are building the world's most engaging, distraction-free video player that forces you to learn.
